# PublicVerdict 🏛️

**An un-complex accountability tracker for political promises.**

This is my college project for tracking whether politicians are actually fulfilling their promises, or if they are just making excuses. Instead of confusing politics, PublicVerdict uses a data-based approach: every promise is tracked, evidence is mandatory, and the public gets to debate and view actual accountability scores.

## How it works:
- **Dashboard:** Tracks all the promises categorized clearly.
- **Evidence:** We rely on hard documents (RTI, news, data) rather than just opinion.
- **Debate Arena:** A structured place where citizens can contest government claims.
- **Scorecards:** Every politician gets a mathematical score out of 100 based on their performance and honesty.
